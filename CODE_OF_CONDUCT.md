# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Cortex welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful.
- Assume good faith.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers.
- Explain disagreements clearly.
- Prioritize project health over personal preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Threats
- Deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without consent

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate these standards.
